A FEW THINGS PARENTS ASK

A little more to know.

Are there colored pages as well as coloring pages?

Yes! The 36-page book pairs 18 fully illustrated colored scene pages with 18 uncolored line-art pages. Children can glance at the colored scene for inspiration, or choose their own completely unique palette.

How do the questions and advice work?

Each spread features an open-ended imagination question (e.g. “What story do you think the bunny is reading?”) and an encouraging thought written directly to the child. It transforms coloring into a warm conversation between parent and child.

Is it suitable for toddlers and children who cannot read yet?

Absolutely. For ages 3–5, a grown-up or older sibling can read the prompt aloud. Children love talking about the pictures and coloring at their own pace.

What coloring tools work best?

Crayons, colored pencils, and gel pens work beautifully. If using juicy alcohol markers, we recommend placing a blank sheet of paper behind the page just in case.

How is the book printed and shipped?

The book is produced and shipped via Amazon KDP (Kindle Direct Publishing). Orders are eligible for fast Amazon Prime shipping with standard Amazon return protections.
